No account snapshot recorded yet.

Hyatt has no public API and this app never asks for your World of Hyatt password. You check the account, the app remembers what it looked like and tells you exactly what moved since last time.

How to read your numbers

  1. Open the World of Hyatt app, or hyatt.com → My Account.
  2. Go to Activity (or My Progress / milestone tracker) for your qualifying night count.
  3. Type those numbers into the snapshot form, or paste the whole activity screen and let the app pull them out.
  4. Do this on a set cadence — every few days is plenty. The app diffs each snapshot against the last one.
