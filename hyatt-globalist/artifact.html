Where to find the numbers

  1. Open the World of Hyatt app, or hyatt.com → My Account.
  2. Go to Activity, or the milestone / progress tracker, for your qualifying night count.
  3. Type the numbers in, or paste the whole screen and let the app pull them out.
  4. Check every few days. Each snapshot is compared against the last one.

This app never asks for your Hyatt password and never signs in for you. Hyatt publishes no member API, so you do the looking and the app does the remembering.
